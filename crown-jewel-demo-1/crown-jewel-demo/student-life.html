Student Life

Life at Crown Jewel School

Clubs, sports, leadership and events that shape the student experience.

Clubs & Societies

Where Interests Grow

Debate Club

Sharpening confident public speaking and critical thinking through regular inter-class and inter-school debates.

Press Club

Home to the school’s young writers and reporters, covering school events and producing our termly newsletter.

Science Club

Hands-on experiments and projects for students curious to explore science beyond the classroom syllabus.

Sports

Active Bodies, Team Spirit

Crown Jewel School fields teams in football, basketball, table tennis and athletics, competing in inter-house and inter-school competitions throughout the year.

Leadership

Growing Tomorrow’s Leaders

Students are given real leadership responsibility through the Student Council, class prefect system, and house captain roles — practical experience in accountability and teamwork.

Events

Celebrating Together

Inter-House Sports

A full day of track and field competition between school houses, one of the most anticipated events of the year.

Cultural Day

A vibrant celebration of Nigeria’s diverse cultures through food, dress, music and dance.

Prize-Giving Day

Facilities

Our Campus

Our campus is equipped to support both academic and extracurricular growth.

Classrooms

Spacious, well-ventilated classrooms designed to support focused learning in small, manageable class sizes.

Computer / ICT Facilities

A dedicated computer laboratory equipped for hands-on ICT instruction across all levels.

Science Facilities

Fully equipped laboratories for Physics, Chemistry and Biology, supporting practical, hands-on science education.

Library

A quiet, well-stocked reading environment supporting both assigned coursework and independent reading.

Sports Facilities

An on-site sports field supporting football, athletics and our termly inter-house competitions.
