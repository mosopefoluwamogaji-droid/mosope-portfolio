About Crown Jewel School

Our Story, Mission & Values

Get to know the community, purpose and principles behind Crown Jewel School.

Our Story

A School Built on Purpose

Crown Jewel School was founded on a simple conviction: that every child deserves an education which develops both character and capability. What began as a single classroom in Victoria Island has grown into a full secondary school, serving families across Lagos who want more for their children than examination success alone — a place where confidence, curiosity and integrity are built alongside academic excellence.

Mission

To provide a nurturing, disciplined and academically rigorous environment where every student is equipped with the knowledge, character and confidence to excel — in the classroom and beyond it.

Vision

To be one of Lagos’s most trusted secondary schools, known as much for the character of our graduates as for their academic achievement.

Core Values

What We Stand For

The principles that guide everything we do.

Excellence

Striving for the highest standard in learning and conduct.

Integrity

Acting with honesty in and out of the classroom.

Discipline

Building consistent, self-directed habits for life.

Respect

Valuing every member of our school community.

Leadership

Growing students who guide and inspire others.

Responsibility

Owning one’s actions, work and community.
